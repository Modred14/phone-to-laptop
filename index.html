Works both ways

Send from your phone to your laptop, or share files from your laptop and download them on your phone.

No internet needed

Files travel over your own Wi‑Fi or hotspot. Nothing is uploaded to a server or stored in the cloud.

Free and open source

No account and no ads. The code is public on GitHub for anyone to read and improve.

Three steps, nothing to set up

If you can scan a QR code, you can use OfflineDrop.

1

Open the app

A window appears with a QR code and an address for your phone.

2

Join the same network

Use the same Wi‑Fi on both devices, or turn on your phone’s hotspot and connect your laptop to it.

3

Scan and send

Scan the code with your phone’s camera, choose your files and tap Send.

Get OfflineDrop

Free for Windows and Mac. You only need the internet to download it.

Windows says the app is blocked or unsafe

OfflineDrop isn’t signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows may warn you. Click More info, then Run anyway, and choose Allow if asked about network access. If Smart App Control blocks it without a way to continue, install Python, download server.py from GitHub and run “python server.py” instead.

Using a Mac

Unzip the download, right-click OfflineDrop, choose Open, then Open again. If it still won’t start, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Is it safe to leave open?

While the app is running, anyone on the same network can send you files or download what you share. Use it on your home Wi‑Fi or your own hotspot, not on public Wi‑Fi, and close it when you’re done.
